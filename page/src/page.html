How the city reads
Every Claude Code session you run is a district: its own floating neighborhood with its own Skybots, each with a name. The more a session works, the more its district builds: a park, a café, shops, apartments, then towers. The Skyborne Loop monorail circles the city and hover cars fly overhead. An amber beam to City Hall means a Skybot needs your approval: answer it on its "Needs you" card, or in the terminal as usual. The beam turns green when you approve and red when you deny.
